# Performance qualification

No category has completed the controlled comparisons required by the
[benchmark contract](benchmark-contract.md). Local measurements show useful
improvements and remaining losses; they do not establish an overall ranking.
The [direct parity audit](parity.md) now includes reproducible counterexamples
and a 16.92× prost CPU loss on deep parse-and-read-all work.

Start with [benchmark results](benchmarks.md) for readable tables and
[benchmark commands](benchmarks/running.md) to reproduce them. The
[earlier qualification notes](benchmarks/qualification-history.md) retain
historical campaigns and their original source pins. Interrupted resource
campaigns are not current running jobs or completed soaks.

The [category register](../bench/scoreboard/categories.json) defines the 52
measurement categories, comparators and corpora. In the table below,
`unmeasured` means no qualified comparative result, even where a local
diagnostic exists. The table is a manual index, not a certification of the
latest commit or CI run.

## Win rules

- **Dev-loop guidance** (SB-03/04): instructions or allocations fall ≥2% on
  targeted cells; no primary cell regresses >1% (2% RPC cells); F
  gates green. These are local comparison defaults; CI remains advisory until
  SB-20 calibrates the thresholds from observed noise. Never leadership.
- **Qualified** (SB-15/22/16): contract §6–§10 — dedicated x86_64
  and arm64 hosts, 5+ randomized paired runs, 95% intervals,
  §7 margins (≥20% CPU/RPC or throughput, ≤5% p99 regression).
- **Wording** (§10.2): every claim names cells, peer versions,
  hosts, budgets, intervals. No universal "fastest" claims.
