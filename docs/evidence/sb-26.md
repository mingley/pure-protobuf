Status on 2026-09-30: SB-26a's corpus qualification and SB-26c's complete
512-cell codec baseline are delivered. RPC and startup/codegen measurement
remain open under SB-26b and its SB-26d/e children. The codec floor and P2
targets are unmet in this dev-loop diagnostic. This is not performance
leadership evidence.

results include that instrumentation. Copy counters do not measure total
prost memcpy activity. This is an instrumented dev-loop baseline.

remove the fresh/read-all losses or close the parent adoption program.
