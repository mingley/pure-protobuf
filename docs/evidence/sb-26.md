present default, typed oneof defaults/absence, exact depths and predicate
kinds, changed last-record bytes and reordered/changed maps. Two complete
inventory runs produced identical JSON. Strict standalone Clippy, format and
fixture regeneration checks pass. The existing codec-bench gate also built and
ran all 21 legacy specimens; its fixed-order timing is not the adoption baseline.
