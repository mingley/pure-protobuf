# Dev-loop advisory comparison: c8dd to 0685

[Benchmarks](../benchmarks.md)

The [dev-loop run](https://github.com/mingley/pure-protobuf/actions/runs/42763730150)
compared base `c8dd4bd7738074a29dee8727814832a4e754198f` with head
`0685d7f6cbc4e0ac9cdfb020bd085a8b7eb5b2be`. The head includes the
[small-response budget change](direct-response-budget-20261110.md). Evidence
validation passed for 1,634 common cells, with none added or removed. The
workflow passed, but its comparison exited **1** and is advisory. Instruction
counts were unavailable; these results do not establish CPU speed or an
overall ranking.

| Reported metric rows | Regressions | Improvements |
|---|---:|---:|
| Syscalls | 275 | 230 |
| Futex-family proxy (`locks`) | 393 | 388 |
| Requested allocation bytes | 1 | 1 |

Eight additional picker `locks` budget rows exceeded a zero limit. Each
reported 0.0005 at both base and head. The comparator printed 1,296 rows
across 822 distinct cells; a row is a metric result, not a unique benchmark
or a win against tonic. The [retained data](devloop-0685-20261110.json)
contains every printed row and the complete evidence validation.

The `locks` field counts process-wide futex-family syscalls, including
setup, wakes and unsuccessful waits. It does not directly measure lock
contention. The eight budget rows cover `least_request`, `outlier_detection`,
`pick_first`, `priority`, `random_subsetting_experimental`, `ring_hash`,
`round_robin` and `weighted_round_robin`. Shared-runner variation limits
interpretation of both syscall metrics.

The two allocation-byte rows are tonic/reference fixtures, not measurements
of the receive change. The tonic/Prost 1,000-entity server-stream fixture
reported 28,250,625.325 → 29,054,411.755 requested bytes per RPC median
(+2.85%). The tonic reference-client/reference-server version reported
29,406,275 → 28,332,596.985 (−3.65%). These process-wide requested allocation
measurements include client and server work; they are not peak live memory.
No allocation-count rows exceeded the comparator threshold.
